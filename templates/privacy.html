Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "us", or "the Service") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our personal expense tracking application.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Data We Collect

Account information: when you create an account, we may collect your name and email address, and store an encrypted/hashed version of your password.

Expense data: information you enter such as amounts, categories, dates, notes, and other details you choose to add.

Usage and device data: basic technical information (such as browser type, pages visited, and approximate timestamps) that helps us operate and secure the Service.

Cookies/session data: small pieces of data stored in your browser to keep you signed in and help the site function properly.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to:

Provide and maintain the Service (for example, saving your expenses and showing summaries).

Authenticate users and protect accounts from unauthorized access.

Improve the app experience by understanding what features are used and fixing issues.

Communicate with you about important updates, support requests, or security notices.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Your information is stored in our application database and related infrastructure used to run the Service. We keep your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data. Some information may be retained where required for legal, security, or operational purposes.

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

5. Third-Party Services

We may rely on third-party providers to host the Service or support core functionality (for example, cloud hosting, error logging, or email delivery). These providers may process limited data on our behalf to perform their services.

We do not share your expense entries with third parties for advertising purposes.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access, correction, or deletion of your information, contact us at support@spendly.app.
